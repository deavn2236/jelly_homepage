双臂示教学习与移动操作Dual-arm imitation learning and mobile manipulation

面向双臂操作技能学习，以主从机械臂采集示教并训练 ACT；仿真与实机演示木块递交、羽毛球抓取和嵌套物体拆解。For bimanual skill learning, leader–follower arms collect demonstrations for ACT; simulation and real robots demonstrate block handoff, shuttlecock grasping, and nested-object separation.

项目背景Background

单次抓取难以覆盖嵌套、勾连和需要双手配合的操作。项目通过人工示教学习动作序列，研究双臂协作在不同物体摆放下的执行方式。A single grasp cannot cover nesting, entanglement, and coordinated two-handed tasks. Human demonstrations provide action sequences for dual-arm manipulation under different object arrangements.

嵌套羽毛球等任务不仅需要选定抓取位置，还需要按顺序固定、分离和递交物体。主从示教记录人如何协调双臂，为学习长动作序列和两臂之间的配合关系提供数据。Nested shuttlecocks require more than choosing a grasp: objects must be held, separated, and handed over in sequence. Leader–follower demonstrations record this coordination to support learning multi-step actions and relationships between the two arms.

任务说明Task

从仿真木块递交开始，扩展到实机单臂抓取、双臂羽毛球拿取和嵌套分离。移动操作平台还考虑装球、球桶安装及简单服务取物等任务场景。Tasks progress from simulated block handover to physical single-arm grasping, dual-arm shuttlecock pickup, and nested-object separation. The mobile platform also considers loading shuttlecocks, installing a tube, and simple service-object handling.

通过主机械臂采集动作、从机械臂执行及相机记录观测，将视觉图像与关节状态、动作序列对齐。验证从单物体抓取扩展到双臂交接与分离时，示教策略能否在对应任务布置中重现动作。Leader-arm actions, follower execution, camera observations, and joint states are aligned into demonstration sequences. Tests examine whether learned actions can be reproduced when progressing from single-object pickup to two-arm handover and separation.

设备说明Robot hardware

双臂移动捡球平台采用智猿底盘、Widow 主机械臂、Viper 从机械臂和 RGB-D 相机。主从结构用于示教数据采集，视觉和关节状态为动作学习提供输入。The mobile platform uses a ZhiYuan base, Widow leader arms, Viper follower arms, and RGB-D sensing. The leader–follower arrangement records demonstrations, while vision and joint state support policy learning.

系统和关键技术System and key technologies

围绕示教数据、动作片段建模和机器人执行组织模仿学习流程，区分仿真验证与实机操作。The imitation-learning pipeline connects demonstrations, action-chunk modeling, and robot execution, with simulation and physical tests presented separately.

  • 主从遥操作与同步数据采集Leader–follower teleoperation and synchronized data：: 同步记录相机图像、关节状态与人工示教动作，建立观测和动作序列的时间对应，为单臂抓取与双臂交接提供训练样本。Record camera images, joint states, and demonstrated actions with temporal alignment, providing examples for single-arm grasping and bimanual handoff.
  • ACT 动作片段模仿学习ACT action-chunk imitation learning：: 用连续动作片段表示操作技能，训练模型从视觉与关节观测预测后续动作序列，并将输出接入机械臂执行流程。Represent manipulation through action chunks, train predictions of subsequent actions from visual and joint observations, and connect the sequences to arm execution.
  • Mask 观测与航点式模仿学习探索Mask observations and waypoint-based learning：: 分别测试目标区域 mask 与航点组织方式，比较不同观测和动作表示对羽毛球拿取、交接及嵌套分离过程的影响。Explore target-region masks and waypoint representations, examining their effects on shuttlecock pickup, handoff, and nested-object separation.

演示与结果Demonstrations and results

补充木块递交仿真、单臂取物、双臂配合及单球、多球、嵌套羽毛球操作片段。视频覆盖不同任务设置，其中多球抓取存在失败案例。Demonstrations cover simulated block handover, single-arm pickup, coordinated two-arm actions, and single, multiple, and nested shuttlecock handling. The slides also document failures in multi-object grasping.
