三维激光雷达与惯性融合导航3D LiDAR–inertial mapping and navigation

面向三维建图与避障，以 LIO-SAM 融合激光雷达与 IMU，衔接规划和控制；实机验证走廊及障碍环境的自主导航。For 3D mapping and obstacle avoidance, LIO-SAM fuses LiDAR and IMU measurements; planning and base control enable real navigation in corridors and obstacle-filled rooms.

2022.09—2023.01Sep 2022–Jan 2023
主要传感器Sensors
三维激光雷达与 IMU3D LiDAR and IMU

项目背景Background

复杂室内环境存在不同高度障碍和连续转弯，需要利用三维几何和惯性信息估计机器人运动，并支持目标点导航。Indoor environments contain obstacles at different heights and repeated turns, motivating joint use of 3D geometry and inertial motion for localization and navigation.

仅依靠平面扫描难以描述悬空、低矮或形状复杂的障碍，连续运动还会改变点云采样时刻对应的姿态。三维激光与惯性融合既提供空间结构，也为运动过程中的位姿连续性提供约束。Planar scans cannot fully describe overhanging, low, or irregular obstacles, while motion changes the pose associated with each point-cloud sample. LiDAR–inertial fusion supplies both spatial structure and constraints on pose continuity.

任务说明Task

采集环境点云并建立地图，持续估计机器人位姿，接收目标位置后生成路径和避障轨迹，驱动底盘到达目标。Build a point-cloud map, estimate the robot pose continuously, and plan obstacle-aware motion to a specified goal.

工作流程由地图建立、位姿估计、目标下发、全局路径与局部轨迹生成组成。演示同时观察机器人实际通行和地图中的位置、路径变化，从而检查感知定位与底盘执行是否对应。The workflow includes map creation, pose estimation, goal specification, and global and local planning. Physical traversal and map-side pose and path updates are observed together to check consistency between estimation and base execution.

设备说明Robot hardware

平台为四轮无人车，搭载三维激光雷达、IMU 和计算控制单元。右侧为独立展示的三维激光雷达传感器。A four-wheel robot carries 3D LiDAR, an IMU, and computing/control hardware. The LiDAR sensor is shown separately to the right.

系统和关键技术System and key technologies

定位建图层估计机器人位姿与环境结构，导航层将地图和目标位置转换为路径与底盘控制。Mapping estimates robot pose and environmental geometry; navigation converts the map and target into paths and base commands.

  • LIO-SAM 激光惯性里程计LIO-SAM LiDAR–inertial odometry：: 融合三维激光点云与 IMU，利用惯性运动先验和激光几何约束持续估计机器人位姿。Fuse 3D LiDAR and IMU measurements, combining inertial motion priors with geometric constraints for continuous pose estimation.
  • 惯性预积分与因子图优化Inertial preintegration and factor-graph optimization：: 通过惯性预积分、激光配准及因子图整合相邻时刻和地图约束，输出供导航使用的连续位姿与点云地图。Combine inertial preintegration, LiDAR registration, and factor-graph constraints to supply navigation with pose estimates and a point-cloud map.
  • 目标点规划与局部避障Goal planning and local obstacle avoidance：: 基于地图生成到目标的全局路径，结合实时障碍信息更新局部轨迹，并把轨迹转换为底盘指令；地图与轨迹可视化用于联调。Plan a global path on the map, update local motion from obstacle observations, and convert trajectories into base commands, using map and path visualization for integration checks.
