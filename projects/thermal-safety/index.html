火力发电智能识别预警系统Intelligent visual inspection and safety warning for thermal power plants

面向电厂设备与作业安全识别，以 YOLO 多类别检测和人脸识别构建验证系统，完成现场样本训练与离线演示。For power-plant safety inspection, YOLO detection and face recognition support a validation system with site-specific training and offline demonstrations.

项目背景Background

火力发电现场包含电气设备、转动机械、高处作业和消防设施等多类对象。设备遮挡、光照变化与远距离小目标，使人工巡查和通用视觉模型都难以稳定覆盖现场风险。项目面向华电莱州电厂的安全生产需求，探索从现场图像中识别设备、防护用品及典型不安全状态，为辅助检查和事件追溯提供结构化信息。Thermal power plants contain electrical equipment, rotating machinery, elevated work areas, and fire-safety facilities. Occlusion, changing illumination, and small distant objects challenge both manual inspection and general-purpose vision models. The project addresses Huadian Laizhou Power Plant’s safety needs by recognizing equipment, protective gear, and potentially unsafe conditions in site imagery. Structured outputs support inspection and event review.

任务说明Task

输入现场采集的设备、作业人员和安全设施图像，输出目标位置、类别和置信度，辅助检查安全设备配置及人员防护情况；人脸比对模块用于探索事件与人员身份的关联。算法研发同时覆盖数据抽帧、标注校验、多类别训练和演示界面集成。Given site images of equipment, workers, and safety facilities, predict object locations, categories, and confidence scores to assist equipment and protective-gear checks. A face-matching module explores identity association for events. Development covers frame sampling, annotation review, multi-class training, and demo integration.

  • 数据覆盖电气设备、热机设备、消防与标识、工器具、防护用品及典型违章场景，重点处理少样本类别与复杂背景。Data cover electrical and mechanical equipment, fire-safety signs, tools, protective gear, and representative unsafe conditions, with attention to rare classes and complex backgrounds.
  • 当前已完成图片级检测与身份比对演示；连续视频告警、跨帧行为判断和全天候在线预警属于后续完善方向。The current demonstrator supports image-based detection and identity matching. Continuous video alerts, temporal behavior reasoning, and round-the-clock monitoring remain further development tasks.

系统和关键技术System and key technologies

以场景数据和检测模型为主线，将数据准备、算法训练、推理输出与 Web 展示连接起来。以下流程图按项目介绍中的实际技术链路整理。The workflow connects site-specific data preparation, model training, inference, and Web presentation. The diagram summarizes the technical pipeline described in the project report.

  • YOLOv11 多类别检测YOLOv11 multi-class detection：: 加载预训练权重，针对电厂图像进行多类别联合训练与参数调整，输出设备、人员及安全标识的检测框、类别与置信度；项目材料记录了 200 余类目标的训练工作。Fine-tune pretrained weights on power-plant images with multi-class training and parameter adjustment. Outputs include bounding boxes, classes, and confidence for equipment, people, and safety signs. The report documents training on more than 200 target classes.
  • YOLO / SAM 辅助标注YOLO / SAM-assisted annotation：: 从原始视频抽帧并过滤冗余画面，以预标注、分割辅助和人工复核构建场景数据。针对稀缺违章类别补充合成样本，并注意合成数据与真实工况之间的域差异。Sample and deduplicate source frames, then combine pre-annotation, segmentation assistance, and manual review. Synthetic examples supplement scarce unsafe-condition classes, while domain differences from real site imagery are considered.
  • 人脸比对与 Flask 集成Face matching and Flask integration：: 将人脸识别与图片上传、推理结果展示集成到 Web 演示系统，探索从视觉识别结果到人员事件追溯的连接。安全帽、口罩和面部遮挡仍会影响身份绑定。Integrate face matching, image upload, and prediction visualization into a Flask-based demonstrator, connecting visual events with identity lookup. Helmets, masks, and facial occlusion remain limitations for identity association.

验证情况Validation

项目介绍记录：灭火器检测在对应验证集上的 mAP@50 为 0.995。该结果仅针对这一类别和验证集，不代表所有违章类别的总体识别率。项目完成了现场数据采集、检测模型训练及图片级演示链路验证；小目标漏检、类别不均衡、跨摄像头泛化和时序误报仍是后续优化重点。The project report records an mAP@50 of 0.995 for fire-extinguisher detection on its corresponding validation set. This is a class- and dataset-specific result, rather than an overall unsafe-behavior recognition rate. Validation covers data collection, detector training, and an image-based demonstration pipeline. Remaining challenges include small objects, class imbalance, cross-camera generalization, and temporal false alarms.
