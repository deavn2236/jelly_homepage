轮腿平衡机器人原型测试Wheeled-legged balancing robot prototype

面向轮腿平台姿态平衡，采用 PPO 强化学习训练控制策略，将状态反馈与执行器控制衔接；实机展示平衡与短距离运动。For wheeled-legged balance, PPO reinforcement learning trains a feedback control policy; the physical prototype demonstrates balancing and short-range motion.

项目背景Background

双轮支撑使机身姿态与运动相互耦合，平台移动和腿部结构变化都会影响平衡。项目采用强化学习训练平衡策略，将连续姿态反馈与机器人执行机构连接起来。With two-wheel support, posture and motion are coupled, and movement or leg-configuration changes affect balance. Reinforcement learning trains a policy connecting continuous state feedback to robot actuation.

任务说明Task

训练轮腿机器人的平衡控制策略，使原型能够保持直立，并在短距离运动中维持姿态稳定。实机演示用于观察站立、姿态变化及移动响应。Train a balance policy that maintains upright posture and stability during short-range movement. The physical clip documents standing balance, posture changes, and motion response.

设备说明Prototype hardware

视频展示双轮与腿部结构组成的机器人原型。The video shows a robot prototype with two wheels and leg structures.

系统和关键技术System and key technologies

以 PPO（近端策略优化）训练平衡控制策略，围绕状态观测、策略更新和原型执行构成控制流程。PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization) trains the balancing policy through state observations, policy updates, and prototype execution.

  • 平衡控制的状态与动作建模State and action modeling for balance：: 将机身姿态、运动变化与轮腿状态作为平衡决策依据，把策略输出映射为平台执行器指令，连接状态反馈和姿态调节。Use body posture, motion changes, and wheel–leg state for balance decisions, mapping policy outputs into actuator commands that close the feedback loop.
  • PPO 策略与价值学习PPO policy and value learning：: 通过交互轨迹更新控制策略与价值估计，利用 PPO 的裁剪目标约束过大的策略变化；训练目标围绕维持直立和运动稳定性组织。Update the control policy and value estimate from interaction trajectories, using PPO’s clipped objective to limit excessive policy changes while optimizing upright stability.
  • 闭环推理与原型调试Closed-loop inference and prototype integration：: 根据当前状态持续进行策略推理并执行动作，结合实机姿态和运动响应检查控制链路，展示轮腿原型上的平衡效果。Infer actions continually from current state and execute them in a loop, checking the integration against observed posture and motion responses on the physical prototype.

演示与结果Demonstration

实机演示展示轮腿原型的平衡姿态和运动响应，用于观察训练策略在机器人上的闭环执行。The physical clip shows balancing posture and motion response, illustrating closed-loop execution of the trained policy on the prototype.
