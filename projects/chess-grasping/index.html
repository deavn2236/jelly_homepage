机械臂棋子视觉抓取Robotic visual grasping of game pieces

面向棋盘上小尺寸棋子抓取，以 YOLO 识别目标、MoveIt 规划机械臂运动；实机展示棋子定位、夹持和搬移。For small game-piece pickup, YOLO detects targets and MoveIt plans arm motion; real demonstrations show localization, gripping, and transfer.

参与类型Involvement
参与项目Collaborative project
操作对象Objects
棋盘与棋子Game board and pieces

项目背景Background

棋子尺寸小、形状相近，棋盘纹理与夹爪接近方向都会影响视觉定位和抓取。任务需要把相机观察到的目标位置转换为机械臂可执行的动作，并处理夹爪与棋盘之间的空间关系。Small, similarly shaped pieces and a patterned board challenge visual localization and grasp approach. The task connects camera-observed targets with executable arm motion while accounting for the gripper’s clearance above the board.

任务说明Task

在棋盘区域选定并定位目标棋子，使夹爪从合适方向接近、夹住并抬离棋盘，随后移动到指定位置。任务重点是小目标抓取和棋盘上方的安全操作。Select and localize a target piece, approach from a suitable direction, grasp and lift it from the board, and transfer it to a destination. The task focuses on small-object pickup and safe motion above the board.

设备说明Hardware

实机包含协作机械臂、末端夹爪和棋盘工作台。视频从近距离观察夹爪与棋子的相对位置及接触过程。The setup comprises a collaborative arm, an end-effector gripper, and a board workspace. A close view documents the relative positions and contact between the gripper and piece.

系统和关键技术System and key technologies

视觉模块负责识别目标，MoveIt 负责将抓取目标转成机械臂运动，夹爪控制完成实际夹持。Vision identifies the target, MoveIt converts the grasp goal into arm motion, and gripper control executes the physical pickup.

  • YOLO 棋子目标检测YOLO game-piece detection：: 从相机画面中识别棋子并获得目标位置，将小尺寸目标与棋盘背景区分，为抓取位姿计算提供视觉输入。Detect pieces in camera images and obtain target positions, separating small objects from the board background for grasp-pose computation.
  • 坐标变换与抓取位姿Coordinate transformation and grasp poses：: 将视觉定位结果转换到机械臂坐标系，结合棋子高度、夹爪开口与接近方向确定末端目标位姿。Transform visual target positions into the arm frame and determine end-effector goals from piece height, gripper opening, and approach direction.
  • MoveIt 逆运动学与运动规划MoveIt inverse kinematics and motion planning：: 根据末端目标求解关节运动并规划轨迹，结合碰撞检查组织接近和抬升路径，处理夹爪与棋盘的空间约束。Solve joint motion for end-effector goals and plan trajectories, using collision checks to organize approach and lifting around board-clearance constraints.
  • 机械臂与夹爪动作衔接Arm–gripper sequencing：: 按预抓取、接近、夹紧、抬升和搬移分段执行，协调轨迹到位与夹爪开闭，使识别结果连接到完整取放动作。Sequence pre-grasp, approach, closure, lifting, and transfer, coordinating arm arrival with gripper actuation to complete the pick-and-place action.

演示与结果Demonstration

实机演示展示棋子视觉抓取过程，包括夹爪对准、接近、夹持和抬升，连接目标定位与机械臂取放动作。The physical demonstration shows visually guided piece pickup, including alignment, approach, gripping, and lifting, connecting target localization with arm manipulation.
