捡球机器人Shuttlecock collection robot

面向堆叠勾连羽毛球拾取，以点云补全、图注意力推理与倾转抽取实现逐个分离；实机困难场景抓取成功率95.1%、清场率96.5%。For nested shuttlecock retrieval, point-cloud completion, graph attention and tilt-and-pull extraction work together; hard-scene robot tests achieve 95.1% grasp success and 96.5% clearing.

任务Task
遮挡、嵌套与勾连羽毛球的逐个分离和清场Sequential extraction and clearing of occluded, nested shuttlecocks
方法Approach
UNNEST：非模态感知、拓扑推理与物理约束操作UNNEST: amodal perception, topological reasoning and physics-aware manipulation
实机平台Robot platform
UR5 · PGE-50 平行夹爪 · ZED 2i 双目相机 · ROSUR5 · PGE-50 parallel gripper · ZED 2i stereo camera · ROS

项目背景Background

羽毛球具有刚性球头与柔性羽裙，多球随机堆叠后会形成侧向嵌套、竖向套叠和反向勾连。球头被遮挡时，仅依据可见羽毛直接夹取，容易压坏羽裙、滑脱，或因锥形接触面的摩擦自锁同时带起相邻目标。项目围绕这类形状相似、存在物理依赖的目标，研究从杂乱堆叠中逐个安全取出的视觉操作方法。Shuttlecocks combine a rigid cork head with a compliant feather skirt. Random piles create lateral nesting, vertical stacking and reverse interlocking. When the cork head is hidden, grasping visible feathers can cause deformation or slippage; friction between nested conical surfaces can also co-lift neighboring objects. The project addresses sequential extraction of visually similar objects with coupled physical dependencies.

任务说明Task

输入工作区的 RGB-D 观测，分离相互接触的羽毛球实例，补全被遮挡的几何结构并确定球头抓取位姿；进一步判断“先取哪一个”，控制机械臂完成接近、夹持、倾转和抽离。每次取球后重新观测场景并更新取球顺序，逐步完成工作区清场。From RGB-D observations, separate touching shuttlecock instances, reconstruct hidden geometry and estimate a cork-head grasp pose. Determine which object can be removed next, then execute approach, closure, tilting and extraction. Re-observe the scene after each attempt and update the removal order until the workspace is cleared.

设备说明Robot hardware

实验平台采用六自由度 UR5 机械臂与大寰 PGE-50 平行夹爪，ZED 2i 双目相机以眼在手上的方式采集近距离 RGB-D 数据。工作站配置 Intel Core i7-12700KF 与 NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3080，通过 ROS 完成感知、决策与机械臂控制模块通信，并配合同步仿真环境检查轨迹的可行性。The platform uses a six-DoF UR5 arm with a DH-Robotics PGE-50 parallel-jaw gripper. An eye-in-hand ZED 2i stereo camera acquires close-range RGB-D data. An Intel Core i7-12700KF / NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3080 workstation runs the perception and reasoning modules, with ROS providing inter-module communication and arm control. A synchronized simulation environment supports trajectory-feasibility checks.

系统和关键技术System and key technologies

UNNEST 将“感知—补全—推理—执行”组织为闭环。完整几何用于确定可抓取球头，交互图用于约束取球顺序，倾转抽取动作用于解除摩擦卡锁，随后通过重新感知处理取球造成的场景变化。UNNEST forms a closed-loop Perceive–Complete–Reason–Act pipeline. Completed geometry localizes graspable cork heads, an interaction graph constrains removal order, and a tilt-and-pull action releases frictional locks. Re-observation handles scene changes after each extraction attempt.

  • 动态卷积实例分割Dynamic-convolution instance segmentation：: 借鉴 ISBNet 的动态卷积机制，以 Sparse U-Net 提取逐点特征，并为不同实例预测专属卷积核与掩码。该无聚类分割策略用于区分物理接触、羽裙交叠且外形相似的羽毛球，再裁剪出各实例的局部点云。Following the dynamic-convolution mechanism of ISBNet, a Sparse U-Net extracts per-point features and an instance head predicts object-specific kernels and masks. Cluster-free segmentation separates physically touching, overlapping and visually similar shuttlecocks before cropping their partial point clouds.
  • 非模态点云补全与 PCA 位姿求解Amodal completion and PCA-based pose extraction：: 采用 PoinTr 式几何感知 Transformer，由可见局部点云恢复包含遮挡球头的完整形状；通过主成分分析（PCA）提取对称主轴，沿主轴定位刚性球头，并约束夹爪闭合方向与主轴正交，生成抓取位姿。A PoinTr-inspired geometry-aware Transformer reconstructs the complete shape, including the hidden cork head, from partial observations. Principal component analysis estimates the symmetry axis; the rigid head is localized along it, and the gripper closing direction is constrained to be orthogonal to that axis.
  • 交互图与 GATv2 取球顺序推理Interaction graph and GATv2 removal-order reasoning：: 以实例的位置、姿态与高度为节点特征，通过高度差和水平邻近关系建立有向约束边。GATv2 进行注意力消息传递并预测各实例的可移除得分，优先取出约束较少的目标；对侧向勾连，结合姿态特征推断相互依赖关系。Nodes encode instance position, orientation and height, while directed edges introduce height and horizontal-neighborhood constraints. GATv2 propagates relational information and predicts removability scores to select a less-constrained target. Pose features also support reasoning about lateral interlocking.
  • Twist-and-Pull 倾转抽取Physics-aware Twist-and-Pull extraction：: 夹紧刚性球头后，以球头为支点施加俯仰倾转，使嵌套羽裙产生几何错位，再配合抽离解除摩擦自锁。动作随嵌套状态调整：松散目标可直接提取，深度嵌套目标先倾转（论文示例约30°），减少相邻羽毛球被共同抬起的情况。After grasping the cork head, apply a pitch tilt about it to misalign the nested skirts, then retract to release the frictional taper lock. Loosely scattered objects can be lifted directly; deeply nested objects receive a tilt first, with about 30° used as an example in the paper, reducing unintended co-lifting.

实验与基线对比Experiments and baseline comparisons

NBD 数据集包含500个随机堆叠场景：羽毛球从50厘米高度落入40×40厘米工作区，并标注实例掩码与6D位姿。按目标密度分为 Easy（5球）、Medium（10球）与 Hard（15球及以上）。端到端实机评估覆盖90个场景、超过1,000次抓取尝试。The Nested Badminton Dataset contains 500 randomly generated scenes: shuttlecocks are dropped from 50 cm into a 40 × 40 cm workspace and annotated with instance masks and 6D poses. Difficulty levels are Easy (5 objects), Medium (10) and Hard (15+). End-to-end robot evaluation spans 90 scenes and over 1,000 grasping attempts.

原文分别报告单次抓取成功率（SR）与场景清场率（CR）。UNNEST 在 Hard 场景下达到95.1% SR、96.5% CR；YOLOv8-Heuristic、Mask3D+CGN 与 AnyGrasp 的 CR 分别为36.5%、55.0%与52.0%。下方直接保留论文主实验原表及各难度结果。The paper reports Grasp Success Rate (SR) and Scene Clearing Rate (CR) separately. UNNEST achieves 95.1% SR and 96.5% CR in Hard scenes; CR is 36.5% for YOLOv8-Heuristic, 55.0% for Mask3D+CGN and 52.0% for AnyGrasp. 感知实验中，Hard 场景实例分割 mAP@50 为89.2%，补全后的 Chamfer Distance 由15.4×10⁻³降至3.8×10⁻³。消融实验显示，移除点云补全、交互图或倾转动作后，Hard 场景 CR 分别降至72.0%、83.9%与86.5%。这些结果对应受控桌面嵌套操作；极端遮挡（可见表面不足10%）和严重形变仍会影响几何恢复与抓取。Hard-scene segmentation reaches 89.2% mAP@50, while completion reduces Chamfer Distance from 15.4 × 10⁻³ to 3.8 × 10⁻³. Removing completion, the interaction graph or the tilt primitive reduces Hard-scene CR to 72.0%, 83.9% or 86.5%, respectively. These results concern controlled tabletop nested-object manipulation; extreme occlusion (less than 10% visible surface) and severe deformation remain challenging.
