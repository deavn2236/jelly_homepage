捡球机器人Shuttlecock collection robot

面向单球与堆叠羽毛球拾取，以 YOLOv5 关键点估计抓握姿态并抖动分离；实机单球/多球准确率97.83%/95.1%。For single and stacked shuttlecock pickup, YOLOv5 keypoints guide grasp poses and shaking separates targets; real grasp accuracy is 97.83% for single and 95.1% for multiple shuttlecocks.

任务Task
羽毛球识别、夹取与分离Shuttlecock recognition, pickup, and separation
感知方法Perception
YOLOv5关键部位检测与几何姿态估计YOLOv5 key-region detection and geometric pose estimation

项目背景Background

羽毛球的球头、羽身和方向差异会改变可夹持位置；多球堆叠或勾连时，直接闭合夹爪容易同时带起多个目标。项目围绕球体姿态判断与抓取后的目标分离，完成自动捡球的视觉与机械臂流程。A shuttlecock’s head, feather body, and orientation affect its graspable region. Piled or entangled targets can be lifted together by a simple gripper closure. The project connects pose-aware perception with post-grasp separation for automatic collection.

任务说明Tasks

识别单球或多球场景中的球头与球身关键部位，估计目标方向和夹持姿态，控制机械臂接近并夹取目标。对堆叠与勾连情况，在抓取后执行摆动、抖动等分离动作，取出目标羽毛球。Detect head and body key regions in single- and multiple-shuttlecock scenes, estimate target orientation and grasp pose, and execute the approach and pickup. Swinging and shaking after closure separate the target from piled or entangled neighbors.

设备说明Robot hardware

实机由机械臂、夹爪、相机与控制计算单元组成，基于 ROS 连接图像识别、抓握姿态计算和机械臂动作执行。The prototype combines a robot arm, gripper, camera, and control computer. ROS connects image recognition, grasp-pose estimation, and arm execution.

系统和关键技术System and key technologies

系统把关键部位检测、几何姿态计算与分步机械臂控制连接起来，解决羽毛球姿态变化和堆叠勾连。The system combines key-part detection, geometric pose computation, and staged arm control to address pose variation and entangled shuttlecocks.

  • YOLOv5 关键部位检测YOLOv5 key-part detection：: 检测球头与球身关键点，获得比普通目标框更适合抓取姿态估计的结构信息。Detect shuttlecock heads and body keypoints, providing structural information for grasp-pose estimation beyond a bounding box.
  • 几何方向与抓握位姿求解Geometric direction and grasp-pose estimation：: 利用关键点相对位置求解方向矢量，选择骨架主干上的抓握点和夹爪方向，将视觉检测结果转成操作位姿。Estimate the direction vector from keypoint geometry and select a grasp point and gripper orientation along the body, converting visual detections into manipulation poses.
  • ROS 分步控制与抖动分离ROS staged control and shaking-based separation：: 按接近、夹紧、抬升和抖动分离组织动作，在夹取后加入摆动以处理多球堆叠和勾连。Sequence approach, closure, lifting, and shaking in ROS, adding post-grasp motion to separate stacked or entangled shuttlecocks.

实验结果Experimental results

实机单羽毛球抓取准确率为97.83%，多羽毛球抓取准确率为95.1%。姿态检测和夹持后抖动分别对应目标选择与堆叠分离环节。Physical tests report 97.83% accuracy for single-shuttlecock grasping and 95.1% for multiple shuttlecocks. Pose detection supports target selection, while post-grasp shaking handles separation.
