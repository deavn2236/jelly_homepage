基于移动操作机器人的厂房自主巡检与设备交互系统Autonomous plant inspection and equipment interaction with a mobile manipulator

面向厂房自主巡检与设备按钮交互，结合移动导航、视觉按钮识别和机械臂接触反馈；在移动操作机器人上完成系统集成与实机演示。For autonomous plant inspection and button interaction, mobile navigation, visual button recognition, and arm contact feedback are integrated and demonstrated on a real mobile manipulator.

项目时间Period
2023.10—2024.10Oct 2023–Oct 2024
本人职责Role
核心开发；按钮识别与机械臂点击Core development in button recognition and arm pressing

项目背景Background

厂房巡检不只涉及观察设备状态，还可能需要机器人主动触发按钮等物理交互。项目将巡检移动与设备操作结合，减少对固定位置机械臂的依赖。Plant inspection can require physical interaction, such as triggering a button. The project connects inspection mobility with equipment manipulation.

不同设备的按钮高度、朝向和可接近空间并不一致，单纯到达导航点不足以完成巡检操作。系统需要把厂房内的移动定位、按钮视觉定位与接触后的动作判断衔接起来。Equipment buttons differ in height, orientation, and accessible space, so reaching a navigation waypoint is insufficient. The system connects plant navigation, visual button localization, and action checks after contact.

任务说明Task

移动机器人到达设备附近，抬升机械臂并扫描环境；检测按钮后计算操作坐标，调整底盘与机械臂位置，依据接触信号执行按压并复位。未检测到按钮时重新调整观察位置。The robot approaches equipment, raises its arm, scans for a button, computes operation coordinates, and adjusts the base and arm. Contact feedback guides pressing and return; unsuccessful detection triggers repositioning.

操作按“移动接近—升降调整—扫描识别—求解操作位置—接触按压—复位”展开。目标不可见时改变观察位置，接触信号用于判断是否进入有效交互阶段，避免将视觉识别成功等同于按钮操作完成。The sequence is approach, lift adjustment, scanning and recognition, operation-pose calculation, contact pressing, and return. Repositioning handles an unseen target; contact feedback indicates entry into the interaction stage rather than treating recognition alone as completion.

设备说明Robot hardware

系统由移动底盘、升降机构、机械臂和视觉传感器组成。底盘承担厂房内移动，升降与机械臂扩大设备交互范围，相机为按钮定位提供输入。The system uses a mobile base, lifting mechanism, arm, and camera. The base provides mobility, the lift and arm extend reach, and vision localizes device buttons.

系统和关键技术System and key technologies

本人负责按钮识别和机械臂点击等核心环节，重点将视觉目标转换为受反馈约束的精确操作。My work focuses on button recognition and precise arm pressing, converting visual targets into feedback-guided manipulation.

  • 按钮视觉检测与操作点定位Visual button detection and target localization：: 识别设备面板中的目标按钮，结合机器人观测与空间位置关系确定按压点，并将视觉结果转换为机械臂可用的目标坐标。Detect the target button on the equipment panel, determine its pressing point from observations and spatial relationships, and transform it into an arm-frame target.
  • 机械臂位姿求解与接近控制Arm-pose computation and approach control：: 围绕按钮位置与面板方向组织末端接近和按压动作，协调底盘站位、升降机构及机械臂工作空间，避免把图像中心直接当作执行目标。Organize approach and pressing around the button position and panel orientation, coordinating the base, lift, and arm workspace when producing executable targets.
  • 接触位姿反馈与状态管理Contact-pose feedback and state management：: 以接触信号判断交互阶段，按扫描、定位、接近、按压和复位切换动作；目标不可见或条件不足时调整观察位置后重试。Use contact signals to track interaction phases and sequence scanning, localization, approach, pressing, and reset, repositioning and retrying when observation or execution conditions are unmet.

演示与结果Demonstrations and results

实机视频展示巡检平台在实验环境中的设备接近与操作过程。流程图给出识别失败重定位、接触检查与操作复位环节。Robot footage documents equipment approach and interaction in the experimental setting. The workflow includes repositioning after failed detection, contact checks, and return.
