RTK 校园道路自主循迹RTK-guided campus route following

面向校园道路自主循迹，自建 RTK 基站并融合 GNSS、九轴 IMU，经 EKF 平滑与 MPC 控制，实现校园道路上的实机循迹和转弯。For autonomous campus-road tracking, a local RTK base, GNSS, and a nine-axis IMU feed EKF smoothing and MPC; real-robot tests demonstrate route following and turns on campus roads.

项目背景Background

普通 GNSS 易受大气、遮挡和多路径影响，位置跳变会干扰底盘循迹。项目搭建差分定位链路，将室外定位和运动控制连接为校园道路导航系统。GNSS errors, occlusion, and multipath can disturb path tracking. Differential positioning links outdoor localization with base control for campus navigation.

校园路线包含较长直道、转弯与坡道，既需要稳定的绝对位置，也需要平滑的短时姿态估计。定位和控制之间的接口需要处理坐标一致性、测量波动和底盘运动响应。Campus routes combine long straights, turns, and ramps, requiring stable absolute position and smooth short-term orientation. The localization-to-control interface must handle coordinate consistency, measurement variation, and base response.

任务说明Task

以山东大学东门停车场至计算机科学与技术学院的路线为目标，建立参考站与车载流动站，记录或设置参考轨迹，控制无人车沿道路行驶。The target route connects the east-gate parking area and the School of Computer Science and Technology. A base station and robot-mounted rover support route recording and autonomous tracking.

将参考轨迹与实时融合位姿放在同一坐标系中，持续计算车辆相对路线的偏离并生成控制指令。通过道路跟拍、路侧视角和参考/实际轨迹对照，观察直行、转弯及坡道阶段的循迹行为。Reference paths and fused poses share a coordinate system so route deviation can be converted into continuous control commands. Following and roadside views, together with reference-versus-measured trajectories, document straight, turning, and ramp behavior.

设备说明Robot hardware

四轮无人车搭载 GNSS 接收器、九轴 IMU、中央控制器、微控制器和轮毂电机；固定参考站向流动站提供差分信息。A four-wheel vehicle carries GNSS, a nine-axis IMU, a central controller, microcontrollers, and hub motors. A fixed base provides differential corrections to the rover.

系统和关键技术System and key technologies

系统按差分定位、融合估计与循迹控制分层，将定位观测持续转化为车辆控制量。The system separates differential positioning, state estimation, and tracking control, continually turning sensor observations into vehicle commands.

  • 自建 RTK 基站与流动站Local RTK base and rover：: 参考站与车载接收机同步观测卫星信号，通过差分信息求解车辆位置，形成校园范围内的绝对定位链路。Synchronize satellite observations at a fixed reference station and vehicle receiver, using differential corrections to obtain absolute positions on campus.
  • GNSS–IMU 扩展卡尔曼滤波（EKF）GNSS–IMU extended Kalman filtering：: 以 GNSS 绝对位置约束惯性漂移，以九轴 IMU 补充姿态变化，通过预测与观测更新平滑位姿，减轻位置跳变对控制的影响。Constrain inertial drift with GNSS position and use the nine-axis IMU for orientation changes; prediction and measurement updates smooth the pose supplied to control.
  • MPC 轨迹跟踪MPC trajectory tracking：: 把融合位姿与参考轨迹放在同一坐标系中，滚动预测后续运动并优化控制量，在直行与转弯过程中修正横向和朝向偏差。Express the estimated pose and reference path in a shared frame, predict future motion over a receding horizon, and optimize controls to correct lateral and heading errors.

演示与结果Demonstrations and results

补充道路车载视角、路侧拍摄与校园坡道等演示，并展示参考轨迹和实际轨迹对比。定位受到遮挡与多路径影响，RTK 定位本身不提供周围障碍感知。Demonstrations include onboard and roadside views, campus routes, and ramps, together with reference-versus-actual trajectories. Positioning remains sensitive to occlusion and multipath; RTK alone does not sense obstacles.
