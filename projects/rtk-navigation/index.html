RTK 校园道路自主循迹RTK-guided campus route following

面向校园道路自主循迹，自建 RTK 基站并融合 GNSS、九轴 IMU，经 EKF 平滑与 MPC 控制，实现校园道路上的实机循迹和转弯。For autonomous campus-road tracking, a local RTK base, GNSS, and a nine-axis IMU feed EKF smoothing and MPC; real-robot tests demonstrate route following and turns on campus roads.

项目背景Background

普通 GNSS 易受大气、遮挡和多路径影响，位置跳变会干扰底盘循迹。项目搭建差分定位链路，将室外定位和运动控制连接为校园道路导航系统。GNSS errors, occlusion, and multipath can disturb path tracking. Differential positioning links outdoor localization with base control for campus navigation.

校园路线包含较长直道、转弯与坡道，既需要稳定的绝对位置，也需要平滑的短时姿态估计。定位和控制之间的接口需要处理坐标一致性、测量波动和底盘运动响应。Campus routes combine long straights, turns, and ramps, requiring stable absolute position and smooth short-term orientation. The localization-to-control interface must handle coordinate consistency, measurement variation, and base response.

任务说明Task

以山东大学东门停车场至计算机科学与技术学院的路线为目标，建立参考站与车载流动站，记录或设置参考轨迹，控制无人车沿道路行驶。The target route connects the east-gate parking area and the School of Computer Science and Technology. A base station and robot-mounted rover support route recording and autonomous tracking.

将参考轨迹与实时融合位姿放在同一坐标系中，持续计算车辆相对路线的偏离并生成控制指令。通过道路跟拍、路侧视角和参考/实际轨迹对照，观察直行、转弯及坡道阶段的循迹行为。Reference paths and fused poses share a coordinate system so route deviation can be converted into continuous control commands. Following and roadside views, together with reference-versus-measured trajectories, document straight, turning, and ramp behavior.

设备说明Robot hardware

四轮无人车搭载 GNSS 接收器、九轴 IMU、中央控制器、微控制器和轮毂电机；固定参考站向流动站提供差分信息。A four-wheel vehicle carries GNSS, a nine-axis IMU, a central controller, microcontrollers, and hub motors. A fixed base provides differential corrections to the rover.

系统和关键技术System and key technologies

系统由 RTK 差分定位、GNSS–IMU 位姿估计与 MPC 循迹控制组成。固定参考站向车载流动站提供差分信息，流动站结合卫星观测求解位置；九轴 IMU 补充姿态与地磁航向，EKF 平滑位姿后交给 MPC，持续修正车辆相对参考路线的偏离。The system combines RTK differential positioning, GNSS–IMU pose estimation and MPC tracking control. A fixed reference station provides corrections to the vehicle rover, which solves its position using satellite observations. A nine-axis IMU supplies orientation and magnetic-heading observations; EKF-smoothed poses feed MPC to continually correct deviation from the reference route.

  • 参考站—流动站 RTK 差分定位Reference–rover RTK differential positioning：: 参考站和车载接收端共同观测卫星信号，参考站生成差分信息并传递给流动站，流动站结合观测与差分信息计算车辆位置，形成校园道路上的绝对定位输入。The reference and vehicle receivers observe satellite signals. Corrections generated at the reference station are transmitted to the rover and combined with its observations to compute the vehicle position, providing absolute localization on campus roads.
  • 九轴 IMU 与地磁航向观测Nine-axis IMU and magnetic-heading observations：: 以九轴 IMU 的姿态及地磁航向信息补充 RTK 位置观测，为车辆转弯过程提供方向信息，使融合估计同时包含位置与朝向。Orientation and magnetic-heading information from the nine-axis IMU complements RTK position observations, supplying directional information during turns so the fused estimate includes both position and heading.
  • 扩展卡尔曼滤波（EKF）位姿平滑Extended Kalman filtering for pose smoothing：: 通过状态预测和观测更新融合 GNSS 位置与 IMU 信息，平滑测量波动，减轻位置跳变对控制的影响，并把融合位姿与参考轨迹统一到同一坐标系。Prediction and measurement updates fuse GNSS positions with IMU information, smooth measurement variation and reduce the effect of position jumps on control. Fused poses and the reference path are expressed in a shared coordinate frame.
  • MPC 滚动优化与闭环循迹Receding-horizon MPC and closed-loop tracking：: 根据融合位姿与参考轨迹计算横向、朝向偏差，在预测时域内优化控制量，将控制指令下发到底盘；车辆运动后继续读取定位反馈，滚动更新直道、转弯及坡道上的循迹控制。Compute lateral and heading errors from the fused pose and reference path, optimize controls over a prediction horizon and send commands to the base. Updated localization feedback then drives receding-horizon corrections along straight sections, turns and ramps.

演示与结果Demonstrations and results

补充道路车载视角、路侧拍摄与校园坡道等演示，并展示参考轨迹和实际轨迹对比。定位受到遮挡与多路径影响，RTK 定位本身不提供周围障碍感知。Demonstrations include onboard and roadside views, campus routes, and ramps, together with reference-versus-actual trajectories. Positioning remains sensitive to occlusion and multipath; RTK alone does not sense obstacles.
