大模型驱动的子母车跨室内外物流配送系统LLM-driven macro–micro robot delivery across indoor and outdoor environments

面向跨室内外配送，以大模型规划与 SLAM、RTK、MPC 协调子母车，实机验证跨尺度运输、交接与末端导航。For cross-environment delivery, language-model planning coordinates macro–micro robots with SLAM, RTK, and MPC; real robots demonstrate transport, handoff, and final navigation.

项目背景Background

室外道路、大厅、窄通道和房间对机器人尺寸与通行能力的要求不同。单一移动平台难以同时兼顾长距离运输与室内末端任务，因此采用大小载具协作衔接不同尺度环境。Outdoor roads, halls, narrow passages, and rooms impose different size and mobility requirements. A macro–micro team links long-distance transport with the final indoor task.

母车具有运输效率和传感器搭载空间，子车具有狭窄区域通行能力；协作的难点在于让两者在合适的位置交接，并保持任务语义、地图位置与执行状态一致。系统因此同时处理语言层面的任务分解和物理层面的跨平台衔接。The larger vehicle offers transport capacity and room for sensors, while the smaller vehicle can enter confined areas. Coordinating their handoff requires consistency between task intent, map locations, and execution state, linking language-level planning with physical cooperation.

任务说明Task

用户通过自然语言提出配送或导航需求，系统将其分解为母车移动、子车卸载、进入目标区域和协同返回等动作。典型任务涵盖大厅至小房间以及室外道路至窄路。The system translates natural-language requests into vehicle movement, micro-vehicle unloading, target-area entry, and coordinated return. Tasks span a large hall to a small room and outdoor roads to narrow paths.

执行过程按照“理解需求—分配载具—前往交接点—卸载与末端导航—返回对接”组织。母车无法进入目标区域时，由子车继续完成末端任务；执行反馈用于检查动作前置条件和计划是否与当前状态匹配。Execution follows request interpretation, vehicle assignment, travel to a handoff location, unloading and final navigation, and return docking. The micro vehicle completes the final stage where the macro vehicle cannot enter; feedback checks action prerequisites against the current state.

设备说明Robot platforms

母车搭载 Robosense Helios 16 三维激光雷达、N100 IMU、ZED-F9P GPS-RTK、车载计算单元和电动滑轨；子车搭载 RealSense 相机、RPLIDAR A3 与车载计算单元。母车负责大范围运输，子车进入狭窄目标区域。The macro vehicle carries a Robosense Helios 16 LiDAR, N100 IMU, ZED-F9P GPS-RTK, onboard computer, and an electric sliding rail. The micro vehicle uses a RealSense camera, RPLIDAR A3, and an onboard computer for access to confined targets.

系统和关键技术System and key technologies

系统分为语言任务规划与双车导航执行两层，由执行反馈连接高层需求和底层控制。The system separates language-level task planning from vehicle navigation and connects them through execution feedback.

  • IF-Planner 反馈规划IF-Planner feedback planning：: 把自然语言需求组织为带前置条件的运动原语，结合规则检查与执行状态修正规划，处理运输、卸载、末端导航和返回之间的依赖。Translate language requests into motion primitives with preconditions, then use rule checks and execution state to revise dependencies between transport, unloading, final navigation, and return.
  • 多传感器定位与 MPCMulti-sensor localization and MPC：: 按场景衔接 RTK、激光/惯性 SLAM 与模型预测控制，将室外大范围运输和室内狭窄区域导航转换为底盘可执行的运动指令。Connect RTK, LiDAR–inertial SLAM, and model predictive control across environments, turning large-area transport and confined indoor navigation into executable base commands.
  • 双车通信与装卸对接Vehicle communication and docking：: 交换两车的定位、任务及执行状态，协调电动载台装卸与视觉–LiDAR 对接，使独立导航模块按交接顺序协同运行。Exchange localization, task, and execution state, coordinating powered loading and visual–LiDAR docking so the two navigation modules follow a shared handoff sequence.

实验Experiments

在 VastHall–TinyRoom 与 OutdoorRoad–NarrowPath 两类真实场景中，按直接/间接表达以及单/多需求形成四类任务。完整系统在两类场景中的成功率分别为 85–88% 和 78–81%。Real-world evaluation covers VastHall–TinyRoom and OutdoorRoad–NarrowPath, with four task types combining direct/indirect expressions and single/multiple demands. Success ranges from 85–88% and 78–81%, respectively.

在间接多需求任务中，加入反馈后，VastHall–TinyRoom 的成功率由 33% 提升至 85%，OutdoorRoad–NarrowPath 由 30% 提升至 78%。独立导航模块测试中，母车导航成功率为 92.7%，子车为 100%。For indirect multiple-demand tasks, feedback raises success from 33% to 85% in VastHall–TinyRoom and from 30% to 78% in OutdoorRoad–NarrowPath. Separate navigation-module evaluation obtains 92.7% success for the macro vehicle and 100% for the micro vehicle.

不同设备的迁移演示Transfer to other robot platforms

将子母车协同流程迁移到不同母车与载台配置，展示运输、装卸、对接及室内行驶。The cooperative workflow is transferred to different macro vehicles and carrier configurations, with transport, loading, docking, and indoor travel.
