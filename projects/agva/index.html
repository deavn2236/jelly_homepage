基于半人型机器人的激光雷达与视觉融合导航操作系统LiDAR–vision navigation and manipulation with a semi-humanoid robot

面向半人型机器人的定位与作业位置到达，融合激光雷达、视觉点云和 SLAM；实机展示走廊导航、绕障与接近工作台。For a semi-humanoid robot, LiDAR, visual point clouds, and SLAM support localization and task-site arrival; real demonstrations cover corridor navigation, obstacles, and workbench approach.

项目背景Background

二维激光雷达只观测一个高度平面，难以覆盖室内不同高度的障碍。项目引入相机信息，并把融合感知与移动操作平台的导航需求连接起来。A 2D LiDAR observes a single plane and can miss obstacles at other heights. Camera information extends perception for a mobile manipulation platform.

移动操作平台的机身与机械臂占据不同高度空间，底盘所在平面安全不代表整机通行安全。融合视觉几何与激光扫描，可为室内地图和障碍代价提供更完整的环境信息。The base, body, and arm occupy different heights, so a clear base-level plane does not guarantee clearance for the whole robot. Fusing visual geometry with laser scans provides richer mapping and obstacle information.

任务说明Task

在复杂室内环境中完成定位、避障和目标位置到达，为后续机械臂作业提供可执行的底盘位置。Localize, avoid obstacles, and reach target locations indoors to provide suitable base poses for manipulation.

在走廊和工作台附近持续定位并生成可通行路径，到达目标后为机械臂操作预留底盘站位。材料分别呈现融合地图、路径规划和语义感知探索，演示以室内导航过程为主。The robot maintains localization and plans traversable routes near corridors and workbenches, ending at a base pose suitable for arm work. The material separately documents fused maps, planning, and semantic-perception exploration, with demonstrations focused on indoor travel.

设备说明Robot hardware

平台采用带升降结构的移动操作机器人，采用二维激光雷达、相机和底盘控制系统。激光和视觉观测共同构建导航所需的几何信息。The slides show a mobile manipulation platform with a lift, 2D LiDAR, camera, and base controller. Laser and camera observations jointly support navigation geometry.

系统和关键技术System and key technologies

以二维激光和视觉点云融合建立几何导航基础，并探索将视觉语义加入代价地图。The system combines 2D LiDAR and visual point clouds for geometric navigation and explores incorporating visual semantics into navigation costs.

  • 视觉点云滤波与投影融合Visual point-cloud filtering and projection：: 将相机点云滤波并投影到导航平面，与二维激光扫描融合，补充单一平面扫描难以覆盖的障碍几何。Filter and project camera point clouds onto the navigation plane, fusing them with 2D laser scans to complement planar obstacle observations.
  • Cartographer 与代价地图Cartographer and costmaps：: 把融合几何输入用于定位建图，依据地图与实时障碍更新导航代价，连接目标点规划和底盘避障执行。Use fused geometry for mapping and localization, update navigation costs from maps and obstacles, and connect goal planning to base-level obstacle avoidance.
  • 深度估计与语义导航探索Depth estimation and semantic-navigation exploration：: 对深度、语义分割及全景分割输出进行对照，研究将物体类别和投影点云映射为导航代价；该部分按探索性工作展示。Compare depth, semantic-segmentation, and panoptic-segmentation outputs and explore mapping classes and projected point clouds into navigation costs.

演示与结果Demonstrations and results

实机视频补充走廊行驶、实验室绕障与接近工作台等不同场景，地图与感知图用于说明导航输入及规划过程。Real-robot clips cover corridor travel, laboratory obstacle avoidance, and approach to a workbench. Maps and perception images explain the navigation inputs and planning.

建图与语义感知结果Mapping and semantic-perception results

图像记录了激光与视觉融合得到的室内地图，以及室内、室外场景的语义感知结果，用于观察环境几何和目标类别的表达。The images record an indoor map from laser–vision fusion and semantic perception in indoor and outdoor scenes, showing the represented geometry and object categories.
