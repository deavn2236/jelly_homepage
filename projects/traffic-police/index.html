交通协警机器人核心算法与系统开发Core algorithms and system development for a traffic-assistance robot

面向路口交通服务，开发导航、违法检测与人脸识别后端，联动信号灯及语音交互；实机展示指挥提醒、问询与遥控移动。Navigation, violation detection and face-recognition backends support intersection services; product footage shows signal-linked gestures, speech queries and remote movement.

核心算法开发者：导航、违法检测、人脸识别及后端设计Core algorithm developer: navigation, violation detection, face recognition and backend design
应用场景Application
城市路口交通疏导与公众服务Urban intersection assistance and public services
系统组成System
轮式平台、双臂与头部、视觉/语音模块、显示屏及业务接口Wheeled platform, arms and head, vision/speech, touchscreen and service interfaces

项目背景Background

城市路口需要持续提供通行指引、交通提醒和便民问询，单一信息终端难以同时完成动作指挥、现场感知和交互服务。交通协警机器人以交警外形的移动平台承载多种功能，通过信号灯、机械动作、语音和显示界面的协同，辅助路口秩序维护与公众服务。Intersections require traffic guidance, reminders and public information. A traffic-assistance robot combines a mobile platform with signal-linked gestures, scene perception, speech and a touchscreen to support intersection management and public services.

任务与本人工作Tasks and personal contribution

本人负责导航、违法检测、人脸识别等核心算法设计与后端开发，将环境感知、目标与身份识别结果连接到机器人业务逻辑。项目整体还包含信号灯联动指挥、语音问询、抓拍曝光、交通业务查询和移动/状态管理。My responsibilities covered core algorithms and backend development for navigation, violation detection and face recognition, connecting perception and identity results to robot services. The overall product also includes signal-linked gestures, speech queries, capture/display workflows, traffic-service access, movement and state management.

  • 交通指挥：无线接入信号灯状态，协调举牌、摆臂、头部动作及语音提示。Traffic guidance: receive signal states wirelessly and coordinate signs, arms, head motion and speech.
  • 感知与业务：支持电动车及头盔相关抓拍、人脸识别、违法提醒和曝光展示。Perception and services: support two-wheeler/helmet-related capture, face recognition, reminders and display.
  • 导航与控制：开展地图定位、目标点导航及后端接口设计，结合遥控、急停和状态反馈组织执行。Navigation and control: develop map localization, goal navigation and backend interfaces alongside remote control, emergency stop and state feedback.

设备说明Robot hardware

实机由轮式移动底盘、可动作双臂与头部、摄像头、麦克风/扬声器、胸前触摸屏和身份证读卡器组成，并配置无线信号接收、GPS、电池和手柄控制等模块。路口信号采集端将灯态发送到机器人，机器人据此组织动作与播报。The robot combines a wheeled base, moving arms and head, camera, microphone/speaker, chest touchscreen and ID-card reader, with wireless signal reception, GPS, battery and handheld control modules. A roadside receiver/transmitter link supplies traffic-light states for coordinated actions and speech.

系统和关键技术System and key technologies

系统将算法与后端服务、交互界面及机器人执行模块连接起来。软件模块文档采用 WPF 与 View / ViewModel / Model 分层结构，分别承载主界面、表情、智能对话、设置及状态管理；导航、视觉识别和信号灯业务通过后端逻辑完成协同。The system connects algorithms and backend services with the user interface and robot execution modules. The module documentation describes WPF with View / ViewModel / Model layers for the home screen, expressions, dialogue, settings and state management, coordinated with navigation, vision and signal-handling services.

  • 导航、违法检测与人脸识别后端Navigation, violation detection and face-recognition backend：: 围绕定位导航、违法检测和人脸识别开展核心算法设计与后端开发，将算法输出组织为机器人服务可使用的结果，并衔接抓拍记录、提醒和曝光展示。Develop the core navigation, violation-detection and face-recognition algorithms and backend, exposing results to robot services and connecting them to capture records, reminders and display.
  • 信号灯读取与动作联动Traffic-light reception and coordinated actions：: 从路口信号读取模块无线接收灯态，控制手臂举牌/放下、头部转动并触发对应语音提示。操作手册记录红灯抬起、绿灯放下的联动方式，并提供手臂切换和停用设置。Receive traffic-light states from a roadside module, drive sign raising/lowering and head motion, and trigger speech prompts. The operating guide records red-light raising and green-light lowering, with arm switching and disabling controls.
  • 视觉检测模块与识别结果集成Visual detection and recognition integration：: 接入合作方提供的 YOLOv3 电动车检测模块，结合头盔抓拍、人脸识别和信号灯状态，由后端组织抓拍记录、识别结果、违法提醒与曝光展示。Integrate the partner-provided YOLOv3 two-wheeler detector with helmet capture, face recognition and traffic-light states, organizing capture records, recognition results, reminders and display in the backend.
  • 科大讯飞语音与智能问答iFLYTEK speech and question answering：: 接入 Iat_record 语音模块和 MyAIUI 智能问答，通过语音唤醒、识别、回答与屏幕展示，为法规、车驾管业务、周边地点及路线提供问询入口。Integrate Iat_record speech and MyAIUI question answering for wake-up, recognition, response and on-screen answers to traffic-service and local-route queries.
  • 状态监测与移动控制State monitoring and movement control：: 整合电量、网络和 GPS 状态，连接防盗告警、手柄遥控及急停控制；设置界面提供手臂停用和关机等功能，支持值守和现场维护。Integrate battery, network and GPS status with theft alerts, handheld movement and emergency stop. Settings include arm disabling and shutdown for operation and maintenance.

定位导航与后端对接Localization, navigation and backend interfaces

导航算法与后端设计结合配套定位导航套件资料展开：利用激光测距与里程计建立二维概率栅格地图，通过实时激光与地图匹配获得位姿，结合目标点进行路径规划与移动控制。下方图像为配套导航套件的架构与界面参考。Navigation algorithm and backend design draws on the supplied localization toolkit: laser observations and odometry build a 2D occupancy grid, scan-to-map matching estimates pose, and configured goals support path planning and movement control. The figures below are architecture/interface references from that toolkit.

  • 地图坐标与目标点管理Map coordinates and goals：: 统一机器人坐标、激光地图和业务地图之间的对应关系，配置地图分辨率、激光外参、起始位姿与目标点，维护导航控制所需的位置和朝向信息。Align robot, laser-map and service-map coordinates, configure resolution, laser extrinsics, initial pose and goals, and maintain position/orientation information for navigation.
  • TCP/IP 与 Protobuf 接口TCP/IP and Protobuf interfaces：: 通过 dataserver 将定位、导航状态与业务软件连接，使用 TCP/IP socket 和 Google Protobuf 组织状态消息、初始位姿及目标指令，使算法服务与机器人主程序解耦。Connect localization/navigation states to the application through dataserver, using TCP/IP sockets and Google Protobuf for status, initial-pose and goal messages.

演示与结果Demonstrations and results

产品演示素材展示了真实路口的指挥动作、公众互动及移动过程，操作手册展示了问询、交通业务入口与手臂设置界面。以下内容用于呈现整机功能与交互流程，现有材料未提供违法检测、人脸识别或导航精度的量化对照数据。Product footage shows intersection gestures, public interaction and movement, while the operating guide illustrates queries, traffic-service access and arm settings. These materials demonstrate product functionality and interaction; quantitative detection, recognition or navigation comparisons are not supplied.
