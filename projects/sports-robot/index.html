三球一体运动机器人Multi-sport robot: table tennis, badminton, and tennis

面向乒乓球、羽毛球和网球对打，融合 YOLO 视觉检测、轨迹预测与协同控制；实机展示移动站位和挥拍回击。For table tennis, badminton, and tennis, YOLO visual detection, trajectory prediction, and coordinated control connect mobile positioning with physical racket returns.

项目背景Background

球类运动要求机器人在来球越过可击打区域前完成感知、判断、移动与回击。不同球类的速度、飞行规律和场地尺度不同，但都需要将来球预测与底盘、挥拍机械臂联动。Ball-sport robots must sense, predict, reposition, and return a ball before it leaves the reachable zone. Different speeds, flight dynamics, and court scales require prediction to be coordinated with base and arm motion.

羽毛球尤其受到反应时间限制：若等到轨迹估计完全稳定后才移动，底盘可能来不及到位。因此采用先预测可击打区域并提前移动、再随视觉观测细化击球点的两阶段思路。Badminton imposes a short reaction window: waiting for a fully stable trajectory estimate can leave too little time to reposition. A two-stage design first predicts an interception region and starts motion, then refines the hit point as observations arrive.

任务说明Task

根据人类来球调整底盘位置、机械臂姿态与挥拍时机，在球体进入可击打空间时完成回击。演示覆盖乒乓球正反手、羽毛球和网球对打，任务关注连续感知与击球执行的衔接。Adjust base position, arm pose, and racket timing from the incoming ball and return it within the reachable interception window. Demonstrations cover table-tennis forehand and backhand, badminton, and tennis.

设备说明Hardware

实机由移动底盘、挥拍机械臂和球拍组成，针对三类球运动使用相应球拍与场地配置。设备图截取自项目演示视频，展示平台与挥拍机构。The physical system combines a mobile base, striking arm, and sport-specific rackets and court setups. The hardware image is a still from the supplied demonstration.

羽毛球感知链路结合双目视觉与球拍端 IMU：视觉提供球体三维位置和轨迹，运动传感补充人类击球瞬间的球拍姿态与速度信息。The badminton sensing pipeline combines stereo vision with a racket-mounted IMU: vision provides 3D ball positions and trajectories, while motion sensing supplies racket orientation and velocity at the player’s stroke.

系统和关键技术System and key technologies

运动控制采用“提前判断运动区域、持续细化击球点”的两阶段结构，将感知预测与底盘、机械臂控制配合起来。A two-stage design estimates an interception region early and continually refines the hit point, coordinating perception with base and arm control.

  • YOLO 检测与双目三维轨迹YOLO detection and stereo trajectories：: 识别球体并获取带时间戳的三维位置序列，结合连续观测估计运动状态，为后续落点和击球点预测提供输入。Detect the ball and obtain timestamped 3D positions, estimating motion state from successive observations for landing and hit-point prediction.
  • SEPNet 视觉–运动融合早期预测SEPNet early vision–motion prediction：: 羽毛球分支融合球体位置/速度和球拍 IMU 姿态/速度，提前预测可击打区域；SEPNet 通过区域分类辅助击球区域中心回归，争取底盘移动时间。For badminton, combine ball position and velocity with racket-IMU orientation and velocity to predict a hit region early. SEPNet uses region classification to assist center regression and gain repositioning time.
  • 最小二乘拟合与 MLP 轨迹修正Least-squares fitting and MLP trajectory refinement：: 随视觉轨迹积累，用最小二乘拟合已观测位置与速度，并以 MLP 估计轨迹模型参数，持续更新后续飞行轨迹和可达击球点。As visual trajectories accumulate, fit observed positions and velocities with least squares and estimate trajectory-model parameters with an MLP, continually updating the flight prediction and reachable interception point.
  • PID 启动与 NMPC 协同控制PID startup and coordinated NMPC：: 前段以 PID 驱动平台向预测区域移动，后段通过非线性模型预测控制协调底盘和机械臂，联合约束击球位置、球拍朝向与击球速度。Start platform motion toward the predicted region with PID, then coordinate the base and arm through nonlinear MPC, jointly considering hit position, racket orientation, and striking velocity.
