SCTNav: Scene-Constrained Monocular Navigation with Ternary Spatio-Temporal Waypoint PredictionSCTNav: Scene-Constrained Monocular Navigation with Ternary Spatio-Temporal Waypoint Prediction

Wendong Guo, Junyi Wang, Yanwei Zheng*, Xiao Zhang, Xingyu Zhang, Dongxiao Yu

IEEE Robotics and Automation LettersIEEE Robotics and Automation Letters中科院2区CAS Zone 2

面向仅用单目 RGB 的图像目标导航，通过三元时空路点预测与场景约束 MPC 连接视觉规划和运动控制；仿真成功率98.0%，三类真实场景平均90.8%。For monocular RGB image-goal navigation, ternary spatiotemporal waypoint prediction and scene-constrained MPC connect visual planning with motion control; success is 98.0% in simulation and averages 90.8% across three real settings.

[Project]

任务说明Task

给定一张目标位置的图像，机器人仅使用单目 RGB 观测导航到目标。机器人需要从视觉序列理解运动和场景结构，并把视觉路点转化为符合自身运动学的速度指令。Given an image depicting a goal location, a robot navigates to it using monocular RGB observations. It must infer motion and scene structure from visual sequences and convert predicted waypoints into velocity commands compatible with its kinematics.

任务中的主要困难包括重复纹理、局部可观测性、环境尺度变化，以及路点预测与真实运动约束之间的偏差。实验覆盖受限房间、长走廊和开放校园。The task involves repetitive appearances, partial observability, changes in environmental scale, and a gap between visual waypoint prediction and physical motion constraints. Evaluation covers confined rooms, long corridors, and an open campus.

主要贡献Main Contributions

  • P3Net 显式区分过去观测、当前画面与未来目标，学习具有不同时间角色的导航信息。P3Net explicitly distinguishes past observations, the current view, and a prospective goal to learn navigation information with distinct temporal roles.
  • SCMPC 从单目序列恢复度量一致的局部三维场景，将障碍物、运动学和轨迹平滑纳入模型预测控制。SCMPC reconstructs a metric-consistent local 3D scene from monocular sequences and incorporates obstacles, kinematics, and smoothness into model predictive control.
  • 将学习式路点预测与显式控制优化结合，并验证 SCMPC 对多种导航策略的适用性。Learned waypoint prediction is paired with explicit control optimization, with SCMPC also evaluated across multiple navigation policies.

方法架构Method Architecture

P3Net 用双向状态空间模型编码过去和当前观测之间的时空关系，再以目标图像为条件，通过交叉注意力融合当前状态与目标相关信息，同时预测距离和未来路点。P3Net encodes spatio-temporal relations between past and current observations using a bidirectional state-space model. Goal-conditioned cross-attention then fuses the current state with target-relevant information to predict distance and future waypoints.

SCMPC 估计序列深度和相机运动，构建度量一致的局部点云与障碍表示。非线性 MPC 依据差速运动学，在目标跟踪、控制平滑与障碍代价之间优化，滚动输出线速度和角速度。SCMPC estimates sequence depth and camera motion to construct local metric point clouds and obstacle representations. Nonlinear MPC follows differential-drive kinematics and balances goal tracking, control smoothness, and obstacle costs to output linear and angular velocities.

实验结果Experiments and Results

训练数据来自七类模拟器或数据集，约含 8.5 万条轨迹和 250 万张 RGB 图像。仿真在未出现在训练集的四种 Gazebo 布局上评估，包含 102 组固定起终点。完整系统获得 98.0% 成功率、2.0% 碰撞率和 95.1% SPL。Training uses approximately 85,000 trajectories and 2.5 million RGB images from seven simulators or datasets. Simulation evaluation uses 102 fixed start–goal pairs across four unseen Gazebo layouts. The full system reaches 98.0% success, 2.0% collision rate, and 95.1% SPL.

在三个未见真实环境中直接部署，不进行微调。每个场景包含 10 条轨迹、每条重复 4 次，共 120 次测试。受限房间、长走廊和开放校园成功率分别为 90.0%、97.5% 和 85.0%，平均为 90.8%。The system transfers without fine-tuning to three unseen real-world environments. Each environment has 10 trajectories repeated four times, totaling 120 runs. Success is 90.0% in confined rooms, 97.5% in long corridors, and 85.0% on an open campus, averaging 90.8%.

P3Net 本身为 13.0M 参数，加入场景几何模块后完整系统为 347.2M 参数。系统在 i7-12700F 与 RTX 3050 的机载平台上运行，D435i 仅启用 RGB，推理无需云端。P3Net has 13.0M parameters; the full system, including scene-geometry estimation, has 347.2M. It runs onboard an i7-12700F and RTX 3050 platform with only the RGB stream of a D435i enabled, without cloud inference.
