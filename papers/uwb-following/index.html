A Novel UWB-Based Full-Range and Multi-Angle Following RobotA Novel UWB-Based Full-Range and Multi-Angle Following Robot

Kun Yu, Wendong Guo, Jun Yan

29th International Conference on Computer Supported Cooperative Work in Design29th International Conference on Computer Supported Cooperative Work in DesignCCF CCCF C

面向近距多角度跟随与远距召回，融合 UWB 引导的 LiDAR 聚类和闭环控制；实机验证0.3–0.7 m跟随与10–30 m召回，直线跟随距离标准差约4.5–4.8 cm。For multi-angle following and long-range recall, UWB-guided LiDAR clustering and closed-loop control support 0.3–0.7 m following and 10–30 m recall on a real robot; straight-line distance standard deviation is about 4.5–4.8 cm.

[PDF][DOI]

任务说明Task

陪护机器人既需要近距离提供辅助，也需要在保持隐私距离时快速响应召唤或跌倒事件。系统需在不同距离与方位下识别跟随目标，并协调近距跟随和远距召回。An assistance robot must provide close support while also respecting a user’s preferred distance and responding to summons or falls. It needs target localization across ranges and angles, as well as coordinated close-following and distant-recall modes.

该系统围绕 0.3–0.7 m 的紧密跟随、±40° 的角度设置和 10–30 m 的远距召回进行实现与测试。The implementation and evaluation cover tight following at 0.3–0.7 m, angular settings up to ±40°, and long-distance recall at 10–30 m.

主要贡献Main Contributions

  • 以 UWB 的目标身份与相对位置引导 LiDAR 点云聚类，提升近距距离估计的稳定性。UWB target identity and relative position guide LiDAR clustering for stable close-range distance estimation.
  • 支持多角度跟随，以适应人机并行、侧后方陪护等相对位置需求。Multi-angle following supports different relative placements for assistance and accompaniment.
  • 把主动召唤、跌倒触发与自动距离调整纳入 ROS 状态机，连接近距和远距模式。A ROS state machine connects close and distant modes through active summons, fall-triggered recall, and automatic range adjustment.

方法架构Method Architecture

近距模式先根据 UWB 测距测角定位标签，再对前方 LiDAR 点云聚类并选择与标签位置一致的目标。控制器根据距离和方位误差调节机器人运动，双 LiDAR 分别服务于目标感知和障碍检测。In close mode, UWB range and bearing locate the tag. LiDAR points are clustered, and the cluster consistent with the tag position is selected. Distance and bearing errors drive closed-loop control; the two LiDARs support target sensing and obstacle detection.

远距模式通过按钮或移动端触发召回，也可由佩戴端 IMU 的跌倒阈值触发。机器人根据 UWB 定位导航至目标，结合障碍物距离调整速度；模式切换统一管理相应参数和功能。Recall is triggered by a button or mobile interface, or by an IMU-based fall threshold. UWB localization guides the robot toward the target, while obstacle proximity adjusts speed. Mode transitions load the relevant parameters and behaviors.

实验结果Experiments and Results

近距实验包含直线与 L 形轨迹，跟随距离设置为 0.3–0.7 m。UWB–LiDAR 方法在直线轨迹上的距离标准差为 0.045–0.048 m，在 L 形轨迹上为 0.043–0.047 m；多角度测试固定距离为 0.5 m。Close-following tests use straight and L-shaped trajectories with target distances from 0.3 to 0.7 m. UWB–LiDAR distance standard deviation is 0.045–0.048 m on straight trajectories and 0.043–0.047 m on L-shaped trajectories. Multi-angle tests use a fixed 0.5 m distance.

远距召回从 10 m 到 30 m 每隔 5 m 测试，每个距离、每种触发方式各进行 20 次独立试验。按钮与跌倒触发在 10–20 m 均为 100% 成功；30 m 时分别为 80% 和 70%，平均召回时间约为 26.5 s。Long-range recall is tested from 10 to 30 m in 5 m increments, with 20 independent trials per distance and trigger. Both button- and fall-triggered recall reach 100% success at 10–20 m; at 30 m they reach 80% and 70%, with mean recall time around 26.5 seconds.
